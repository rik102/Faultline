Ambiguous action wording

Distracted and low-tech users interpret “Continue” as a final payment action.

Fix: Rename to “Review plan” or “Continue to payment setup.”

Trust collapse risk

Auto-renewal and conditional refund language appears after payment fields, increasing anxiety.

Fix: Move renewal and refund terms before the form in plain language.

Cognitive overload

Three parallel calls to action compete with the checkout form.

Fix: Make one primary path and demote secondary actions.

Exploit probing

Scammer persona targets promo/support code and vague refund policy.

Fix: Add rate limits, audit logs, and explicit eligibility checks.
